About the League

Real soccer.
Real community.

Toca SF was built for players who already live here. We run a structured, competitive-but-friendly coed league on weeknights — full turf surface, lit for evening play, centrally located in San Francisco.

Register a full team or sign up as a free agent. Either way, there's a spot for you.

Get on the list
⚽
8v8 Coed Format
Half-pitch games, 50 minutes each. Fast, fun, and open to all skill levels.
📍
Central Location
Games are played at a centrally located SF turf field — walkable, lit, and accessible by public transit.
🏆
Refs + Playoffs
Every game has a certified referee. Season ends with a single-elimination playoff.
🌎
Bilingual — Se Habla Español
Registration, outreach, and support available in English and Spanish.
Community First

Built for the
neighborhood.

Toca SF is committed to making the game accessible to everyone in San Francisco. We know cost is a real barrier to participation for many players — so we built a scholarship fund into the league from day one.

Players can sign up as free agents and apply to play completely free. No hoops, no hassle.

20%
of net profits go directly to the Toca SF scholarship fund — covering full season fees for SF residents who apply and demonstrate financial need.
Sign up as a free agent and indicate you'd like to apply for a scholarship
Priority given to residents of underserved SF neighborhoods including the Tenderloin and Civic Center
Spanish-language support available throughout — hablamos español
How it Works

Simple to join.

01
Register your team or as a free agent
Sign up online. Full teams need a minimum of 10 players (coed). Free agents get placed on a team by us.
02
Pay your registration fee
Team registration fee covers the full season. Scholarship players play free — apply during registration.
03
Show up Thursday nights and play
Show up on game night, get your ref, and play. Season ends with a single-elimination playoff.
